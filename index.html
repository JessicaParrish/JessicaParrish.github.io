    Some Of My Projects:

    CookSync Web App

    UX/UI Design

    CookSync is a UX/UI concept project designed to solve one of the most common frustrations in everyday cooking — finding a recipe shouldn't be harder than making one. The app combines a fluff-free recipe browsing experience with an AI ingredient matcher, letting users search by what they already have at home rather than what they still need to buy.

    Taniti WGU Course Project

    UX/UI Design

    Taniti is a UX/UI WGU course project designing the official tourism website for a small tropical island looking to grow its visitor base. Starting from a research brief and ending with a nine-page high-fidelity prototype, the project followed a full UX process — including two rounds of guerrilla usability testing — built around one core insight: travelers don't book trips to destinations they don't trust.

    Vibrational Earth Redesign

    UX/UI Design

    Vibrational Earth is a local body and skincare business in Niles, Ohio with a website that wasn't doing it justice. I redesigned the homepage from the ground up — fixing a broken mobile layout, restructuring the navigation, and building a page that actually reflects the quality of the business.
